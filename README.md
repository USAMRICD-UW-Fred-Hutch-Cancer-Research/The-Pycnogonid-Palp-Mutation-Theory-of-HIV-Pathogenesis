# The Pycnogonid-Palp Mutation Theory of HIV Pathogenesis

A theoretical research repository investigating speculative structural, evolutionary, or biomimetic intersections between the morphology of pycnogonid (sea spider) palps and mechanisms of cellular binding or mutation dynamics in viral pathogenesis.

## Project Overview
This project provides a centralized framework to analyze, model, and document the **Pycnogonid-Palp Mutation Theory**. The core objective is to evaluate whether specific chemosensory or structural adaptations found in marine arthropod appendages offer novel insights into viral cell entry, receptor targeting, or genetic mutation vectors.

## Repository Structure

*   `DISCLAIMER.md` — Project scope and research limits.
*   `DISCLAIMER_GENERAL_REFERENCE.md` — Academic and literature reference notes.
*   `DISCLAIMER_GENERAL_WELLNESS.md` — General wellness and safety disclaimers.
*   `EXPORT_CONTROL.md` — Regulatory guidelines for open-source scientific code.
*   `W26 Week 5.docx` / `.pdf` — Weekly progress documentation and analytical briefs.

## Core Research Focus
*   **Structural Comparative Analysis:** Mapping tactile/chemosensory receptor profiles of pycnogonid palps against viral glycoprotein structures.
*   **Biomimetic Modeling:** Utilizing computational simulations to assess alternative models of membrane fusion and cell-penetrating dynamics.
*   **Evolutionary Pathogenesis:** Cataloging genetic mutation vectors that mimic ancient marine arthropod cellular mechanics.
